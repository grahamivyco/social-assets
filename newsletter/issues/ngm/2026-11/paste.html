TECHNIQUE SPOTLIGHT

Blackwork

One color of thread, counted stitches and a small motif repeated until it reads like lace. Stitchers in Tudor England wore it on their collars and cuffs, and it's still one of the most satisfying things to watch grow on a piece of linen.

Want to try it? The Daytime Counted Thread & Needlepoint group meets on the second Tuesday. Bring a question and someone will happily point you to a first pattern.
