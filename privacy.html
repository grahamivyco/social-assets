Privacy Policy

Open Frame Design · Last updated October 20, 2026

Open Frame Design ("Open Frame", "we") is a design and marketing agency in Minneapolis and Barcelona. This policy explains what we collect when you message or comment on our Instagram account (@open.framedesign) or our Facebook Page (Open Frame Design), and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • The messages you send us and comments you leave on our posts
  • Your public name or username, and the ID Instagram or Facebook gives us for your account
  • Whether you follow our account, as reported by Instagram
  • Your email address, only if you choose to send it to us

How we use it

  • To reply to your messages and comments, including automatic replies to common questions
  • To keep track of project inquiries so we can follow up
  • To send our newsletter, only if you gave us your email and asked to join

We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI models.

Where it is stored

Messages are processed by our own automation software and logged in a private Google Sheet that only Open Frame can access. Meta's own terms and privacy policy also apply to anything you send through Instagram or Facebook.

How long we keep it

We keep message logs for up to 24 months, then delete them. Newsletter emails are kept until you unsubscribe.

Deleting your data

To have your information deleted, send us a message on Instagram or Facebook that says "delete my data", or contact us through https://openframe.design/links. We will delete your messages, account ID, username and email from our records within 30 days and confirm when it is done. To unsubscribe from the newsletter, reply "stop" or use the link in any email.
