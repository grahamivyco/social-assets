# Open Frame signature style (approved 2026-11-03: today's new look, with Claude's suggestions; test on next raw footage)

Graham Ivy, Instagram [@open.framedesign](https://www.instagram.com/open.framedesign/). Not approved yet: after his notes it moves to `Signature Style/open-frame/` on the Mac, and it counts as approved only after it's been tested on his real footage.

## What's already decided (from REEL-RULES.md, design-notes.md and his feedback)

- Talking heads only for now; extras become graphics, not extra footage.
- Original footage color. No grading unless he asks; never the old heavy filter.
- No frame or rim around the screen and no logo on the video.
- Captions: a few words at a time (3), Manrope ExtraBold, white, the word being said in yellow #F4BF3A, no outline, shadow or box.
- Hook title (Graham picked C, color field): a solid brand-color band across the top third, edge to edge, huge Helvetica Neue Bold words (up to 3 lines), accent word in yellow; drops in, words land one by one, slides back up. No tilt, circle, outline or sparkle.
- Key point graphics: centered card in a brand color with wide art plate tiles, words underneath; the website glyph shows openframe.design. A real screenshot when the line is about a real thing, mixing sizes: inset, almost full screen with padding, or behind the head.
- On-screen links and the end card say openframe.design.
- Colors: ink #111110, cream #F3F1EA, red #F24333, blue #2F6BF0, yellow #F4BF3A, green #33AC66. Flat, no shadows or gradients.
- Trending audio added by him in the app; export voice-only. Posts as an Instagram Trial Reel.
- Covers: big photo of him, full-bleed, no frame or rim, no captions.

## Not observed yet (needs 3 to 5 of his approved Reels)

Pacing (cuts per 10 s, pause length kept), caption height on screen, how often a graphic appears, transition types, and camera punch-ins. These get measured frame by frame from his examples, then filled in here.

## Suggested (his call)

- One graphic about every 6 to 8 s, never two in a row without his face in between.
- Captions at about 62% of the height so they clear his chin and the Instagram buttons.
- A hard cut on most line changes, with one shape wipe per Reel at the turn of the idea.
